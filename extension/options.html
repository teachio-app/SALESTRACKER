DeskTracker Market Capture

Saves the Sales Tracker pages you open into your own tracker.

Your DeskTracker deployment, with /api/market/ingest on the end. The value of MARKET_INGEST_TOKEN. Not your cron secret — a different one, on purpose.

This reads only pages you open yourself. It never browses, never requests a page you did not ask for, and sends nothing anywhere except the URL above. Open the browser console on a Sales Tracker page to see each capture as it happens.
